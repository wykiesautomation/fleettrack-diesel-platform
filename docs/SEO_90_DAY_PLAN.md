# 90-Day SEO Growth Plan

## Days 1–14: Technical foundation
- Deploy public landing pages, canonicals, sitemap, robots controls and structured data.
- Submit the sitemap and request indexing.
- Verify that login, dashboard, devices, billing, admin and API pages remain out of search.
- Capture performance and indexing baselines.

## Days 15–30: Product proof
- Add original screenshots from Mobile Tracker 1.
- Publish a real explanation of GPS drift, route outliers and matched routes.
- Add a phone-tracker versus dedicated GPS tracker comparison.
- Add clear author/company and support information.

## Days 31–60: South African topical authority
- Publish practical pages on Android background tracking, POPIA-aware tracking, fleet route history and 4–20 mA monitoring.
- Add internal links from every article to the matching commercial page.
- Seek legitimate links from partners, pilot customers, local business directories and industry associations.

## Days 61–90: Conversion and expansion
- Improve titles and descriptions for pages with impressions but weak click-through rates.
- Add verified pilot outcomes and FAQs.
- Expand only into queries that Search Console proves users are already discovering.
- Measure trial registrations and device connections, not rankings alone.

## Success gates
- All priority pages indexed.
- No private customer pages indexed.
- First branded and long-tail clicks recorded.
- At least three target terms reach page one before chasing broader head terms.
