Privacy & POPIA notice

AssetTrack 360 may process account details, contact information, IP and security logs, device identifiers, registration tokens, GPS location and journey history, sensor readings, alerts, billing records and support communications. Information is used to provide, secure, support and improve the platform, authenticate users and devices, deliver alerts, maintain records and meet legal obligations.

Information may be handled by authorised hosting, communications, payment and technical service providers where necessary to operate the service. Access is limited according to role and operational need. Records are retained only for legitimate operational, contractual, security or legal purposes and are deleted or anonymised when no longer required, subject to backups and lawful retention duties.

Lawful tracking only: AssetTrack 360 may only be used to monitor assets, devices or persons where the user has ownership, authority, consent or another lawful basis. Covert, unlawful or unauthorised tracking is prohibited.

Terms of Use

  • Users must provide accurate account information and protect login details, API tokens and devices.
  • Users are responsible for obtaining all permissions and lawful authority required for tracking, monitoring and data collection.
  • The service may not be used for stalking, covert surveillance, unlawful interception, harassment, fraud, system abuse or infringement of third-party rights.
  • GPS, network and sensor readings can be delayed, inaccurate or unavailable because of signal, hardware, power, configuration or third-party services. The platform must not be the sole safety-critical control.
  • Access may be limited or suspended to protect users, systems, legal rights or service integrity.
  • Subscription, hardware, cellular, installation and specialist engineering charges may be governed by the applicable quotation, order or plan.
  • South African law applies unless a written agreement states otherwise.

Cookie Policy

The platform uses essential cookies and similar storage for sign-in sessions, security, preferences and reliable operation. Optional analytics or marketing technologies should only be enabled with appropriate notice and consent. Blocking essential cookies may prevent login or secure platform functions.

Data Rights

Subject to applicable law and identity verification, a data subject may request access to personal information, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, a copy or export, withdrawal of consent, or information about processing. Registered users can also revoke devices, pause tracking and request account closure. Some records may be retained where required by law, security, dispute or contractual obligations.

PAIA and information access

Requests for records under the Promotion of Access to Information Act must identify the requester, the requested record and the right being exercised or protected. Any applicable PAIA manual, request form, fees and lawful grounds for refusal or redaction will apply. Contact support for the current request process.
