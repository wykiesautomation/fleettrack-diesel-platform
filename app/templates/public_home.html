Why the name?

More than tracking. A complete view of every asset.

AssetTrack 360 brings identity, location, condition, operation, connectivity, alarms and history together. The platform is designed around the physical asset and the work that depends on it, not around one tracker, sensor or controller.

From a vehicle on the road to a borehole pump on a farm, AssetTrack 360 brings every connected asset into one secure operational view.

Connected Hardware & OT Signals

Connect protected field signals, supported ESP32 hardware and existing industrial control systems through approved interface modules and read-only gateways.

  • ESP32-WROOM-32 / ESP32D 38-pin supported
  • ESP32-S3 and ESP32-C3 profiles planned
  • 4–20 mA via isolated signal conditioner
  • 0–10 V via protected voltage conditioner
  • Digital inputs, pulse counters, MQTT and Modbus
  • Read-only OPC UA gateway for ABB and Siemens systems planned
Protected input requiredNever connect 4–20 mA, 0–10 V, 5 V, 12 V or 24 V directly to an ESP32 GPIO. ESP32-S3, ESP32-C3 and vendor-specific OPC UA profiles remain roadmap items until production validation is complete.
