# Universal Device Visuals and WROOM Alignment

- Adds a normalized percentage and responsive progress bar to universal analog channel groups.
- Adds digital ON/OFF indicators and pulse total captions.
- Applies LIVE, SIMULATED and WAITING quality presentation across capability-aware cards.
- Uses WROOM1 as the production firmware baseline and advances it to firmware 1.6.5.
- WROOM simulation telemetry is now tagged SIMULATED, while physical outputs remain OFF in Simulation.
- Pin map remains GPIO34 AI1, GPIO27 DI1, GPIO26 Pulse1, GPIO25 DO1, GPIO32 Local Arm and GPIO33 Wi-Fi LED.
